About ONEEIGHTY.GAMES

oneeighty.games is a free darts trivia site for fans of the PDC (Professional Darts Corporation) circuit. Every day there is a new question, and a handful of quick games built around the players, the rankings and the history of the sport.

What you can play

The Top 10
A new question each day, such as "Last 10 players to lose a PDC World Championship semi-final". Name all ten answers before you run out of lives.
Higher or Lower
Is the next player ranked higher or lower than the last one in the PDC Order of Merit? Build the longest streak you can.
Line Them Up
Put five players in the right Order of Merit order.

Where the information comes from

Questions and rankings are put together by hand from publicly available PDC results, rankings and statistics. The Order of Merit games show the date they were last updated. We try hard to be accurate, but mistakes can happen. If you spot one, please tell us.

Who is behind it

The site is made by a darts fan who wanted a daily game for fellow fans. It is an independent fan project and is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the PDC or any player.

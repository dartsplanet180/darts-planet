How to play

The Top 10

Pick a day from the list. Each day has one question with ten correct answers, ranked from 1 to 10, for example the last ten winners of a particular tournament. Type a player's name and choose them from the suggestions. If the player is on the list, their name appears in the right slot with their ranking number. If not, you lose a life. You have three lives. Name all ten without losing a life and you score a whitewash. If you run out of lives you can reveal the answers.

Higher or Lower

You are shown a player and their current PDC Order of Merit rank, plus a second, hidden player. Decide whether the hidden player is ranked higher (closer to number 1) or lower. A correct guess adds to your streak and the hidden player becomes the next one to beat. One wrong guess ends the run. If two players share the same rank, either answer counts as correct. Your best streak is saved on your own device.

Line Them Up

Five random players appear. Tap them in order from highest ranked to lowest ranked in the Order of Merit. A correct tap places the player and reveals their rank. A wrong tap costs one of your three lives. Clear as many rounds as you can.

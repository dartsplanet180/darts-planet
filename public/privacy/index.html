Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026

ONEEIGHTY.GAMES is a free darts trivia website. You do not need an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address or any other personal details to play.

Information we do not collect

We do not store your game results on our servers. We do not sell personal data.

Cookies and local storage

The site itself does not set cookies. It saves your best streaks for Higher or Lower and Line Them Up in your browser's local storage, so they are still there next time you play. This stays on your own device and is not sent to us. You can clear it at any time in your browser settings.

Advertising

We may show ads provided by third parties such as Google AdSense. Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show ads based on your visits to this and other websites. You can manage personalised advertising at adssettings.google.com or aboutads.info. Where required, such as in the EU and UK, you will be asked for your consent first.

Analytics and hosting

We use the web analytics provided by our hosting provider, Netlify, to count visits, see which pages are viewed and roughly where visitors are located. It works from server logs, so no tracking code or cookies are placed on your device and we cannot identify you from it. Netlify may process technical data such as IP addresses to produce these statistics. See Netlify's privacy policy for details.

Children

The site is not aimed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy from time to time and will change the date above when we do. Questions? Email dartsplanet180@gmail.com.
